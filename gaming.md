The footage already exists. The home is already in there.

Decades of home videos, real estate walkthroughs, Christmas recordings, and family archives contain enormous amounts of spatial data — room layouts, furniture positions, item locations, wall configurations — that nobody ever extracted.

The Video to Digital Twin concept uses AI vision to watch that footage and output HL-structured data: rooms identified, objects located, addresses assigned, vault pages generated.

You don't need to have been running HL when the footage was recorded. The system can go back in time and build the record retroactively.

**The result:** A navigable digital twin of any space captured on video — even spaces that no longer exist.
