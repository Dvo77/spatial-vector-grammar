The HL vault is a perfect RAG document set.

Every space page, item page, and kit page is structured markdown with consistent YAML frontmatter. Load it into a local LLM — Ollama, LM Studio, anything — and you have an AI that knows your space in full detail, runs offline, and never sends your data to a cloud.

Ask it: "Where is my tap and die set?" It answers from your vault. Ask it: "What do I have at GR-WS-S2?" It lists everything at that address. Ask it: "What haven't I touched in three years?" It cross-references your access logs and tells you.

No API key. No subscription. No latency. No privacy concerns.

**The edge processing concept** extends this to computer vision: point a camera at any labeled surface, the local model reads the HL addresses, and updates the vault in real time. The system becomes self-maintaining.
