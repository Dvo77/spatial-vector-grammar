Every HL address is a coordinate in a persistent world.

The Gaming concept uses the HL grammar as the backbone for real-world game mechanics: quests tied to physical locations, inventory systems that mirror your actual tools, achievements for completing organization tasks, multiplayer barter and trade using the HIL Exchange.

The physical space becomes a game map. Labeling a shelf is a quest completion. Finding a missing item is a discovery event. Passing a tool to someone with full provenance is a trade transaction.

For younger family members, this makes the system engaging rather than a chore. For developers, it's a game engine backend that runs on markdown files and QR codes.
