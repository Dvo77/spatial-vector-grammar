The HL grammar is a foundation. These are the things you can build on top of it.

Some of these are in development. Some are waiting for the right builder. All of them are free and open to contribute to.

---

| Concept | What It Is |
|---|---|
| [Living Archive](/concepts/living-archive/) | Your home as a navigable historical record |
| [House Brain](/concepts/house-brain/) | The smart home layer built on HL addresses |
| [Human OS](/concepts/human-os/) | Personal knowledge and life management through HL |
| [Living Provenance](/concepts/living-provenance/) | Items that carry their own history |
| [Temporal Digital Twin](/concepts/temporal-twin/) | Walk through any space at any point in time |
| [Video to Digital Twin](/concepts/video-twin/) | Extract spatial data from existing footage |
| [Edge Processing](/concepts/edge-processing/) | Local-first AI with HL context |
| [Gaming](/concepts/gaming/) | HL as a real-world game layer |
| [Garden App](/concepts/garden-app/) | Spatial tracking for outdoor and growing spaces |
| [Artist Reward](/concepts/artist-reward/) | Provenance and attribution for creative work |

---

*Have an idea for a new concept? Open an issue or PR on [GitHub](https://github.com/Dvo77/spatial-vector-grammar).*
