An HL address tells you where something is right now.

A Temporal Digital Twin tells you where everything was at any point in history — and lets you navigate that space as if you were there.

The concept: HL addresses with a timestamp suffix create a four-dimensional map of any space. You can reconstruct your grandfather's factory floor from 1963. You can walk through the house you grew up in. You can see what was in every room on any date the system was running.

The data already exists in most cases — home videos, real estate photos, family photos, insurance records. The Temporal Digital Twin concept provides the structure to link it all into a navigable timeline.

**Buildings survive their own demolition.** The factory that came down last year still exists in the record, fully navigable, fully documented — if someone thought to map it.
