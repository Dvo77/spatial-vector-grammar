The same grammar that addresses your garage can address your knowledge, your projects, your relationships, and your time.

The Human OS concept extends HL beyond physical space into the full topology of a person's life:

- **Skills and knowledge** get HL-style identifiers
- **Projects** have addresses in your personal namespace
- **Relationships and context** are linked to items and spaces
- **Time** becomes a coordinate — any record can be located in both space and when

The result is a personal operating system that doesn't live in any single app. It lives in structured text files, in a vault, in a GitHub repo — wherever you keep things. The grammar is the infrastructure, not the software.
