All tools run in a browser. No install. No account.

---

## Label Tools

Make physical tags for items, zones, and spaces.

**[Label Engine](/tools/label-engine)** — Build and print HL-formatted labels for any item or location.

**[Sign Maker](/tools/sign-maker)** — Generate printable zone signs with address codes.

**[Cart Label Generator](/tools/cart-label-generator)** — Batch labels for mobile carts and rolling storage.

**[HIL Exchange](/tools/hil-exchange)** — Import and export label sets in HL format.

---

## Mapping & Space Tools

Assign addresses to your physical environment.

**[HL Super Tool](/tools/hl-super-tool)** — All-in-one: address builder, label output, zone sign, and wiki page in one tool.

**[Room Code Generator](/tools/room-code-generator)** — Generate structure and zone codes for any room or building.

**[Room Mapper](/tools/room-mapper)** — Lay out a space and assign anchor columns visually.

**[Scanner](/tools/scanner)** — Scan QR codes and look up HL addresses on the spot.

---

## Record & Data Tools

Build your inventory and document what you own.

**[Batch Mode](/tools/batch-mode)** — Process large item lists and generate addresses in bulk.

**[Estate Package](/tools/estate-package)** — Document a home, shop, or estate collection for transfer or archive.

**[Starter Pack](/tools/starter-pack)** — First-time setup. One space, one shelf, one label. Start here.
