Dr. Piper's screws are in a drawer labeled OLG 1.

But the label only tells you they're there. It doesn't tell you who he was, what he built with them, or why they matter.

Living Provenance is the concept that every item in an HL system carries a record — not just a location, but a history. Origin, ownership, repairs, context, meaning.

When an item moves, the record moves with it. When it's sold, gifted, or inherited, the provenance transfers. The object carries its own story.

This is what makes estate handling honest. This is what makes a tool from a grandfather more than a tool. This is what lets someone find an item at an estate sale, read its record, and return it to the family.

**The format:** A provenance page linked to an HL item ID. Plain markdown. Readable by any human or AI. Permanent as long as the file exists.
