---

Most homes are organized for right now. The HL Living Archive treats your home as a record of everything that ever happened in it.

Every item has a history. Every zone has a timeline. Every change is logged — not as a burden, but as a byproduct of normal use.

The result: your home becomes a navigable archive. You can ask "what was in this room in 2019?" You can trace where something came from and where it went. You can see the full provenance of anything in your space.

**The core idea:** The same address system that tells you where things are today also tells you where they were yesterday — and why they moved.

This is what makes estate handling, inheritance, and historical documentation possible without any special effort. The record builds itself as you use the system.
